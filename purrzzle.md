# Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 26, 2026

Arion built Purrzzle as a mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

Purrzzle is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone data, or other sensitive personal information.

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

- Game progress, such as completed levels, puzzle state, cat-related progress, food balance, and selected language.
- Purchase-related records, such as purchased product IDs, purchase tokens, and purchase time, only to unlock and restore in-app content.

This information is used only to provide gameplay, save your progress, and restore purchased content.

## In-App Purchases

Purrzzle uses Google Play Billing to offer in-app purchases.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

Google may collect and process purchase-related information according to Google’s own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or analytics providers.

Purchase processing is handled through Google Play Billing, which may involve Google processing purchase-related information as described in Google’s privacy policy.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play services for in-app purchases. These services may collect information as described in their own policies.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Storage and Retention

Game progress and purchase records are stored locally on your device. Some app data may also be included in Android system backup if backup is enabled on your device.

Local app data remains on your device unless you delete the app data or uninstall the app. Purchase records associated with your Google Play account may remain available through Google Play so that purchases can be restored.

## Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app’s storage in your device settings.

## Children’s Privacy

Purrzzle is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete the information.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect locally stored app data. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
